# Coin photograph sources

US photographs: United States Mint. Euro photographs: European Central Bank coin gallery.

Photographs were resized and their exterior backgrounds removed for the 3D display. The coin artwork itself was not generated or redrawn. Euro pairs show a national design and the shared value side; they do not claim to photograph one particular physical specimen.
