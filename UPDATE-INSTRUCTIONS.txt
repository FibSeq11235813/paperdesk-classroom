Paperdesk — wider default view and dynamic shared work

Replace server.mjs in your repository root, and the seven files in public/.
Leave vendor assets alone. Deploy, then hard-refresh teacher and student pages.
No environment changes are required; the server adds its storage column automatically.
Deploy between sessions because Free Render deployments reset temporary data.

The default frame is 1.3 times wider/taller (one zoom-out step). Click Standard
slide view on the teacher side to replace an older saved crop. Students get
the new default automatically for 24 x 12 inch PDFs.

New PDF slide shares include annotation vectors and use the original revealed
native PDF page. In Class panel > Shared work, open a share. Use +/- and Fit
page to inspect it, and scroll to navigate. Old shares and graph shares still
use image previews, which can also be zoomed and snipped.

To copy work: open your teacher PDF, open a student share, choose Snip to my
workspace, then drag a rectangle. The preview closes. Click on the teacher
page to place the copy, or drag right to choose its width. Escape cancels.
Snips are high-resolution PNGs (up to 2400 pixels along the longest side),
not editable vector paths. Undo removes a placed snip; the partial eraser
leaves snippets intact. They appear on the clean screen and in PDF exports.
